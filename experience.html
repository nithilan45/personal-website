01

devrev — gtm / partnerships

I was on the partnerships team at DevRev, next to PBMs who’d been doing B2B GTM for years. That’s where selling started to make sense. Pitching a dreamy product doesn’t get you there. You sell a specific outcome: what changes for the customer, what gets unblocked, which number moves. After that, the work was concrete. Deal risk. Mapping bugs. A renewal sitting in the wrong region.

Most of my time went to pipeline hygiene across AMER, APJ, and EMEA. I automated it with SQL analytics and internal AI agent workflows so at-risk opportunities didn’t stay buried. That surfaced 13+ at-risk opps, including a $900K+ renewal. I also fixed 56 misrouted opportunities, $1.2M+ in pipeline.

On the partner side I shipped a TypeScript partner-portal snap-in that repaired LATAM region mapping and defaulted opportunity owners by region. About 12 deals had been stuck on bad routing. That unblocked them. I also helped run $4K in LinkedIn ads.

02

haiintel — forward deployed engineering

At HaiIntel I was a forward deployed engineering intern. I built AI tools for a hospital client across 5 workflows, for clinicians and staff with real patients and real schedules. They didn’t have time for a tool that only looked smart in a demo.

I indexed 8K+ patient records and cut retrieval from about 10 minutes to under a minute. Someone was usually waiting on that record mid-shift, so the latency was the product. I also automated 300+ appointment follow-ups a month. That’s where you find out what happens when automation fails and nobody notices.

The work had to fit a system that already existed. Measure what the workflow costs people in time, ship the glue that makes the model useful, and stay until the hospital team trusts it with you out of the room.
