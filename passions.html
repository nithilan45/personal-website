passions

I’m into a bunch of different things, and I like the places they overlap.

01

philosophy

I like reading different schools and taking the parts that change how I actually live. Primary sources, not the summary. The Myth of Sisyphus is probably my favorite. Right now it’s Epicureanism and Absurdism.

02

travel

I’ve been to ten countries so far, and that number is going up. What I care about is talking to people who see things differently. The conversation I still think about was with a nun in Assisi. It moved a few things I thought were settled.

03

cricket

I played from 9 to 15, and I’ll watch it for the rest of my life. As a kid I represented the Western United States, after being picked as a top-30 player in the country, and I captained Dallas regionally. I was a wicketkeeper-batsman. Batting was the part I loved. I still pick up a bat when I’m overthinking. It settles me. If I have a free hour I’ll put a game on, any format, any teams. Cricket also gave me my closest friends, the ones I grew up playing next to. I wouldn’t be the same person without it.
