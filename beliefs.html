beliefs

Here are a few contrarian things I strongly believe, that people have disagreed with me about. They’re mine, and they’re core to who I am.

meritocracy sports longevity

01

meritocracy

Modern-day meritocracies are extremely flawed. They cultivate artificial pursuits and performativeness. People “perform” for colleges, investors, employers, and other external sources of validation. Rather than working on things that genuinely matter to them, they work on what looks impressive to those judging them.

02

sports

As life gets safer, sports matter more, psychologically. They’re one of the few places left where tribal conflict, physical courage, domination, sacrifice, and status can show up honestly. Take competition out of childhood and people don’t get less competitive. It just moves somewhere murkier, where nobody will admit there’s a fight: admissions, careers, status, social media.

03

longevity

Longevity escape velocity could cause an economic motivation crisis. Time is valuable because it runs out. If people think they have centuries to build a career, take a risk, or make money, the urgency goes, and so does a lot of the work and investment that depends on a finite horizon. You can add years of human potential and still lose the pressure that turns potential into output. Capitalism runs on that pressure.
